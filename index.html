We operate a dedicated 9.4 ft × 8.4 ft digital LED advertising screen at GAR InfoBahn, Kokapet, Hyderabad — an elite corporate ecosystem spanning 6 towers with 16 floors each, totaling 96 floors of premium commercial workspace.

What makes this location compelling is its audience density. The campus sees an average footfall of around 1 lakh people every single day, translating to an estimated 3 million monthly footfall. This is not general public traffic; it is a highly concentrated corporate workforce spending their day within a premium business environment.

The screen is positioned directly at a central movement point traversed by employees across all towers, creating repeated daily physical exposure that reinforces digital and omnichannel campaigns. Because multiple companies operate on 24/7 rotational shifts, audience movement continues actively throughout the day and into the night.

Beyond the permanent workforce, the campus sees continuous external footfall from ongoing recruitment drives, vendor meetings, and client visits, accompanied by approximately 5,000 daily cab movements. This generates constant brand impressions across employees, leadership, visitors, and transit passengers entering or leaving the campus.

We can operate the screen for up to 20 hours per day, tailoring loop frequencies and schedules to match target campaign dynamics. We also offer dedicated screen ownership for agreed durations, providing complete freedom to showcase a brand's product lines or manage agency client portfolios in an uncluttered, exclusive media environment.
